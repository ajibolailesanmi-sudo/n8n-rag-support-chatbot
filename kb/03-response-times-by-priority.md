# Response times by priority

We set a priority on every ticket when we triage it. The priority decides how quickly a technician responds.

| Priority | What it means | Response time |
|---|---|---|
| Priority 1 (Critical) | The whole office or a critical system is down, or there is a security incident | Within 30 minutes |
| Priority 2 (High) | One person can't work, or a key system is degraded with no workaround | Within 2 business hours |
| Priority 3 (Normal) | A problem with a workaround, or a how-to question | By the end of the next business day |
| Priority 4 (Request) | Planned changes, new user setups and equipment orders | Scheduled within 3 business days |

## When the 30-minute Priority 1 response applies

- Business and Premium plans: 24/7.
- Essentials plan: during help desk hours. Priority 1 problems reported after hours are handled at the start of the next business day.
- Security incidents: 24/7 on every plan, including Essentials.

## What "response" means

A response means a technician has started working on your ticket and contacted you. It is not a promise of when the problem will be fixed, because that depends on the problem.

## Getting the right priority

Tell us how many people are affected and whether anyone can't work. If you think we set the wrong priority, reply to the ticket and say why, and we will review it.
