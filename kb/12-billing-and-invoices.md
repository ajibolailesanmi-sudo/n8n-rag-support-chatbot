# Billing and invoices

## When you are billed

Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month for that month's services. We email them to your billing contact, and they are also in the client portal.

## Paying

- Payment is due within 30 days of the invoice date.
- We accept ACH bank transfer (preferred) and credit card. Card payments carry a 3% processing fee.

## Adding or removing users

- Users added during the month appear on the next invoice, with a prorated charge for the part of the month they had.
- Removed users stop being charged from the next invoice.

## Late payments

If an invoice is 15 days overdue we send a reminder. If it is 30 days overdue, a late fee of 1.5% per month may apply.

## Questions and changes

- To question an invoice, raise a billing request in the client portal within 30 days of the invoice date.
- To change your billing contact, an authorized contact for your company can request it in the client portal.
