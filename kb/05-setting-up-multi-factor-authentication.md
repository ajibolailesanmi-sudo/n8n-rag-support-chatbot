# Setting up multi-factor authentication

Every account must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Microsoft Authenticator app. Text message (SMS) codes are not allowed.

## Set up MFA

1. Install Microsoft Authenticator on your phone from the App Store or Google Play.
2. On your computer, go to aka.ms/mfasetup and sign in with your work email.
3. Choose to add the Authenticator app and scan the QR code with your phone.
4. Approve the test notification by entering the number shown on your screen.

## Getting a new phone

- If your old phone still works, add the new phone at aka.ms/mfasetup before you reset or trade in the old one.
- If your old phone is lost or broken, contact the help desk. We confirm your identity by calling you back on the number we have on file, then reset your MFA so you can set it up again.
- If a lost phone had company email or files on it, report it the same day so we can remove company data from it.

## Unexpected sign-in requests

Never approve a sign-in request you didn't start, even if it keeps appearing. Deny it and report it straight away as a security incident: it usually means someone has your password.
