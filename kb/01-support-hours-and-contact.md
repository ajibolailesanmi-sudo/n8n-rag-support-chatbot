# Support hours and how to reach us

## Help desk hours

Our help desk is staffed Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time. We are closed on US federal holidays.

## Ways to reach the help desk

- **Client portal:** submit a ticket at any time. Tickets submitted outside help desk hours are picked up the next business morning, unless they are Priority 1.
- **Email:** use the help desk email address on your welcome sheet. Every email becomes a ticket.
- **Phone:** use the help desk number on your welcome sheet during help desk hours.
- **Chat assistant:** this assistant answers questions from our help articles 24 hours a day. It can pass your conversation to a person when it can't help.

## Urgent problems outside help desk hours

On the Business and Premium plans, call the emergency line printed on your support card for Priority 1 problems at any time, day or night. Priority 1 means the whole office or a critical system is down, or there is a security incident.

On the Essentials plan, Priority 1 problems reported after hours are handled at the start of the next business day. Security incidents are the exception: they are handled 24/7 on every plan. See "Reporting phishing or a security incident".

On the Premium plan, the help desk answers problems of every priority 24/7.

## What to include in a ticket

Tell us who is affected, what you were trying to do, any error message (a screenshot helps), and how many people can't work. This helps us set the right priority.
