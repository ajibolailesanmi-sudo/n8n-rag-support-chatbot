# Connecting to the VPN

## Do you need the VPN?

You only need the VPN for the office file server and the apps that run in your office. Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint) works anywhere without the VPN.

## Which computers can connect

We use the FortiClient VPN app, which we install on every company laptop. Personal computers can't connect to the VPN.

## Connect

1. Open FortiClient.
2. Choose the connection named "Office".
3. Sign in with your work email and password, then approve the prompt in Microsoft Authenticator.

## If it won't connect

- Check that the internet works by opening any website.
- Quit FortiClient completely and open it again.
- Hotel, airport and café Wi-Fi sometimes blocks VPN connections. Try your phone's hotspot instead.
- Still stuck? Submit a ticket in the client portal with a screenshot of the error message. If nobody in the office can connect, call the help desk, because that is a Priority 1 problem.
