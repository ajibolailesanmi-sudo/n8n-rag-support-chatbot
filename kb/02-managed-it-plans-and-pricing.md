# Managed IT plans and pricing

We offer three managed IT plans. Prices are per user per month.

## Essentials: $65 per user per month

- Help desk support during help desk hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern)
- Remote support
- Security updates (patching) for computers and servers we manage
- Antivirus on every managed computer
- Microsoft 365 administration: accounts, licences, mailboxes and permissions

## Business: $95 per user per month

Everything in Essentials, plus:

- After-hours support for Priority 1 problems, 24/7
- Microsoft 365 backup (email, OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams)
- A quarterly technology review with your account team

## Premium: $140 per user per month

Everything in Business, plus:

- 24/7 support for problems of every priority
- 4 hours of onsite visits per month
- A dedicated account manager

## Good to know

- Every plan has a minimum of 5 users.
- Plan prices do not include hardware or software licences. Microsoft 365 licences are billed separately at Microsoft's price.
- A one-time onboarding fee of $500 per office covers the setup and documentation of your systems. It is waived on 24-month agreements.
- Microsoft 365 backup can be added to the Essentials plan for $4 per user per month.

For contract terms, upgrades and cancellation, see "Changing plans or cancelling".
