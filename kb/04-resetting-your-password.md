# Resetting your password

## Reset it yourself

If you set up your security info when you enrolled in multi-factor authentication, you can reset your own Microsoft 365 password at any time at passwordreset.microsoftonline.com. Follow the steps and approve the check on your phone.

## Locked out after too many attempts

Your account unlocks automatically after 15 minutes. Wait, then try again or reset your password yourself.

## Ask the help desk

If self-service reset doesn't work, contact the help desk. For your security, we confirm your identity by calling you back on the phone number we have on file before we reset anything. We will never ask for your current password, by phone, email or chat.

## Password rules

- Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. A passphrase of four or more unrelated words is easier to remember.
- Passwords do not expire on a schedule. Change yours straight away if you think someone else has seen it, and report it as a security incident.
- Don't reuse your work password on any other website.
