# Onsite visits

## When we come onsite

We fix most problems remotely. Onsite visits are for hardware, network equipment, printers and office moves.

## Service area

We cover offices within 50 miles of our office. Outside that area, travel time is billed at the onsite rate.

## Booking and rates

- Standard onsite visits are scheduled within 2 business days. Book them through the client portal.
- Essentials and Business plans: $125 per hour, with a 1-hour minimum.
- Premium plan: 4 hours of onsite visits per month are included. Extra hours are $110 per hour.

## Emergencies

For Priority 1 problems on the Business and Premium plans, a technician can be onsite within 4 hours during help desk hours.

## Office moves

Tell us at least 4 weeks before an office move. We quote office moves separately.
