# Ordering laptops and equipment

## Standard laptop

Our standard laptop is a business-class 14-inch model with 16 GB of memory, a 512 GB SSD and a 3-year next-business-day warranty. We quote the current price when you order.

## How to order

1. Submit an equipment request in the client portal and say who the device is for.
2. We send a quote within 1 business day.
3. After you approve the quote, the laptop arrives in 5 to 7 business days, already set up with the person's account and apps.

## Setup fee

Setting up a new computer costs $150 per device on the Essentials and Business plans. It is included on the Premium plan.

## What we support

We support computers we supplied or approved. Personal computers are not supported. Work email and Teams on a personal phone are fine through the Outlook and Teams apps.

## Old devices

We securely wipe and recycle old company devices for $25 per device, and give you a certificate of data destruction.
