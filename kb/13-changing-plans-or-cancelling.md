# Changing plans or cancelling

## Your agreement

Every agreement starts with a 12-month initial term. After that it renews month to month.

## Upgrading

You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time. The new price applies from the next invoice.

## Downgrading

You can move to a lower plan at the end of the initial term, or in any later month, with 30 days' notice.

## Cancelling

- Give 30 days' written notice by submitting a cancellation request in the client portal. It must come from an authorized contact for your company.
- If you cancel during the initial 12-month term, an early termination fee applies: 50% of the monthly fees remaining in the term.

## If you leave

We help you move to your next provider. That includes handing over admin accounts and passwords securely and helping export your data, up to 5 hours at no charge.
