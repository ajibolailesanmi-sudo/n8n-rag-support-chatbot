# Reporting phishing or a security incident

## A suspicious email you didn't click

In Outlook, choose Report, then Phishing. This sends the email to our security team and removes it from your inbox. Don't forward it to colleagues.

## If you clicked, opened or approved something

Call the emergency line on your support card straight away, at any time of day, if you:

- clicked a link or opened an attachment in a suspicious email,
- typed your password into a page you weren't sure about, or
- approved a sign-in request you didn't start.

Security incidents are Priority 1 on every plan, 24/7, and a technician responds within 30 minutes.

## While you wait

- Disconnect the computer from the network: turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable.
- Don't turn the computer off, because that can destroy evidence we need.
- Don't try to fix it yourself or run cleanup tools.

## What we do

We reset your password, sign you out of every device, check your computer and mailbox for anything left behind, and tell you what we found and whether anything else is needed.
