# Adding a new employee

## When to tell us

Submit a new employee request in the client portal at least 3 business days before the start date. If the new employee needs a new laptop, allow 5 business days.

## What to include

- Full name and job title
- Start date
- Manager and department
- Shared mailboxes, folders and apps they need
- Whether they need a laptop

## What we do

We create the Microsoft 365 account, assign licences (billed to you at Microsoft's price), prepare multi-factor authentication enrollment, add the person to the right groups and shared mailboxes, and set up their laptop if one was ordered.

Setting up a new user is a Priority 4 request and is included in every plan at no extra charge. Laptops have their own setup fee; see "Ordering laptops and equipment".

## The first day

The new employee receives a welcome email with first sign-in steps. They can book a free 15-minute remote orientation with a technician through the client portal.
