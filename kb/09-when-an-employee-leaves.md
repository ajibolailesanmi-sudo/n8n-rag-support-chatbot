# When an employee leaves

## Tell us early

Submit an employee departure request in the client portal as soon as you know the leaving date. Requests received before 3:00 p.m. Eastern on a business day are completed the same day. For an urgent removal, for example when someone is dismissed, call the help desk.

## What we do

1. Block sign-in, sign the person out of every device and remove their MFA methods.
2. Convert their mailbox to a shared mailbox for 30 days and give their manager access. After 30 days we delete it, unless you ask us to keep it.
3. Keep their OneDrive files for 30 days and move anything the manager needs into the manager's OneDrive.
4. Remove their licences so you stop paying for them.

## Company laptops

Return company laptops within 5 business days. We wipe them securely and get them ready for the next person.

## Billing

A removed user stops being charged from the next invoice. See "Billing and invoices".
