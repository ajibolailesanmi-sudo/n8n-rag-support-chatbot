# Email on your phone

## Use the Outlook app

Use the Microsoft Outlook app for iPhone or Android. The phone's built-in mail apps are not supported and can't connect to your work mailbox.

## Set it up

1. Install the Company Portal app and sign in with your work email. This enrolls the phone so it can receive company email.
2. Install Microsoft Outlook and sign in with your work email.
3. Approve the prompt in Microsoft Authenticator.

## Personal phones

You can use a personal phone for work email and Teams through the Outlook and Teams apps. Company data in those apps is protected, and if the phone is lost or you leave, we can remove company data without touching your photos or personal apps.

## Shared mailboxes

To get access to a shared mailbox, ask through a ticket in the client portal and include your manager's approval. Access is set up within one business day and the mailbox appears in Outlook automatically.
